= connectome-fs becomes its own org
:description: The graph filesystem substrate moves under github.com/connectome-fs — Dev-Centr, HCI-Nerdz, and OpenShellOrg stay partner audiences, not owners of the VFS.
:revdate: 2026-08-09
:keywords: news, org, github, substrate

The implementation belongs with systems work: editions, GUID nodes, associations, and the adapters that will follow (file-manager plugins, projection layers, drivers).

Partner orgs keep their lanes:

* **Dev-Centr** — developer / git / agent-workspace practice
* **HCI-Nerdz** — cognitive and surface implications
* **OpenShellOrg** — shell query and hierarchical projection

This site and the Antora docs are the canonical public face for the substrate itself.
